# Stuart Carr-Gomm - Enhanced Research Document

## Subject Name & Credentials

**Stuart Carr-Gomm** - British historian, author, and expert on ancient British monuments, particularly stone circles and henges. Former registrar of the Alexander Keillman Museum. Known for making archaeology accessible to general audiences through clear writing and engaging presentations.

## Core Philosophy/Teachings

### Connection to Ancient Landscapes

**Ley Lines and Sacred Geography:**
- Exploration of alignment theories in ancient monuments
- Connection between landscape features and spiritual significance
- Understanding how ancient peoples oriented their sacred sites
- Integration of astronomy and geography in megalithic architecture

### Stone Circle Interpretation

**Purpose and Function:**
- Ceremonial and ritual uses of stone circles
- Astronomical alignment theories
- Community gathering and social cohesion functions
- Connection to ancestor veneration and death rituals

### British Folklore and History

**Cultural Continuity:**
- How ancient practices influenced later British folklore
- The survival of pre-Christian beliefs through medieval period
- Connection between landscape features and mythological narratives
- Role of sacred sites in cultural memory

## Key Works

- **"The Story of Stone Circles"** - Comprehensive guide to megalithic monuments
- **"The Encyclopedia of Stone Circles"** - Reference work on British stone circles
- **"Mysteries of Ancient Britain"** - Exploration of prehistoric British sites
- **"The Stone Circles of Britain, Ireland and Brittany"** - Detailed survey of megalithic monuments
- **"Prehistoric Avebury"** - Study of the Avebury henge complex
- **"The Celts"** - Introduction to Celtic history and culture
- **"The Revolt of the Puritans"** - Historical study of 17th century England
