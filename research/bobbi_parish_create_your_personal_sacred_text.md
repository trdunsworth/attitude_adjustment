# Research Document: Bobbi Parish - Create Your Personal Sacred Text

## Overview
Bobbi Parish's "Create Your Personal Sacred Text" offers a structured, five-phase process for crafting a living spiritual document that reflects one's core values, beliefs, and spiritual journey. This aligns with the Attitude Adjustment project's goal of integrating spiritual/psychological practices into daily self-development.

## Core Practice
**Crafting a personal sacred text as a living spiritual document** - Not a static artifact, but an evolving companion that grows with you. It serves as:
- A mirror for self-discovery
- A compass for decision-making
- A legacy document for loved ones
- A ritual tool for meaning-making

## Five-Phase Framework

### Phase 1: Excavation (Identifying Core Values)
**Purpose**: Uncover the foundational values, beliefs, and experiences that shape your spiritual DNA.
**Methods**:
- Spiritual autobiography timeline
- Peak experience analysis
- Values sorting exercises
- Ancestral/inherited belief audit
- Shadow work integration

**Key Questions**:
- What moments have felt most "sacred" or meaningful?
- What values do I return to when under pressure?
- What did I inherit vs. what did I choose?
- Where do I feel most aligned? Most fragmented?

### Phase 2: Articulation (Naming & Framing)
**Purpose**: Give language to the ineffable; create vocabulary for your spiritual landscape.
**Methods**:
- Personal credo writing
- Defining key terms in your own words
- Creating a personal glossary of sacred concepts
- Metaphor and image banking
- Drafting "I believe" statements

**Key Questions**:
- What words carry weight for me?
- How would I explain my spirituality to a stranger?
- What metaphors best capture my experience?
- What language feels alive vs. dead?

### Phase 3: Embodiment (Ritual & Practice)
**Purpose**: Move from cognitive understanding to lived experience through ritualized practice.
**Methods**:
- Designing personal rituals for daily/weekly/seasonal cycles
- Creating sacred space (physical or temporal)
- Body-based practices (breath, movement, sensation)
- Threshold rituals for transitions
- Integration practices for difficult emotions

**Key Questions**:
- What does this value look like in action?
- How does my body know this truth?
- What small daily act embodies this?
- Where do I need ritual support?

### Phase 4: Integration (Weaving into Life)
**Purpose**: Make the sacred text functional in real-world contexts - relationships, work, decisions, crises.
**Methods**:
- Decision-making frameworks based on core values
- Relationship covenants
- Work/vocation alignment mapping
- Crisis navigation protocols
- Legacy and ethical will components

**Key Questions**:
- How does this guide my choices?
- Where am I living in alignment? Out of alignment?
- What agreements do I need with others?
- What do I want to leave behind?

### Phase 5: Evolution (Living Document Maintenance)
**Purpose**: Establish rhythms for review, revision, and deepening over time.
**Methods**:
- Quarterly/annual review rituals
- Version control with dated entries
- Crisis-triggered revisions
- Community witness/sharing (optional)
- Succession planning for the document

**Key Questions**:
- What has shifted since last review?
- What needs to be released? Added? Deepened?
- How has life experience tested these values?
- Who holds this with me?

## Practical Entry Points for Daily Journal

### Micro-Practices (5-10 min)
- **Morning**: Single value intention setting
- **Evening**: Alignment review (Where did I live my values? Where didn't I?)
- **Weekly**: One Phase 3 ritual deepening

### Monthly/Quarterly
- **Monthly**: Values audit (rate alignment 1-10 across life domains)
- **Quarterly**: Phase 5 review - revise one section of sacred text

### Annual
- Full sacred text revision ceremony
- Legacy document update
