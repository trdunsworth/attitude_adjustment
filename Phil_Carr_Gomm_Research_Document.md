# Phil Carr-Gomm - Enhanced Research Document

## Subject Name & Credentials

**Philip Carr-Gomm** (born 1952) - British author, psychologist, and former Chosen Chief of the Order of Bards, Ovates & Druids (OBOD) from 1996 to 2021. Trained in psychology at University College London and studied at the Tavistock Clinic. Known for making Druidry accessible to modern seekers through clear writing and practical guidance. Author of numerous books on Druidry, nature spirituality, and the natural world.

### Seasonal Celebrations (from OBOD tradition)

**Samhain (October 31)**
- Honor ancestors and the dead
- Reflect on what needs to be released
- Tell stories of those who have passed
- Create an ancestor altar

**Yule (Winter Solstice)**
- Celebrate returning light
- Reflect on seeds of intention
- Create winter decorations
- Gather with community

**Imbolc (February 1)**
- Honor Brigid and early spring
- Clean and refresh spaces
- Plant seeds (literal or metaphorical)
- Notice early signs of spring

**Ostara (Spring Equinox)**
- Balance activities with rest
- Celebrate new beginnings
- Plant gardens
- Create spring decorations

**Beltane (May 1)**
- Celebrate with flowers and greenery
- Dance around maypoles or fires
- Honor relationships
- Enjoy outdoor activities

**Litha (Summer Solstice)**
- Stay up to watch sunrise
- Gather herbs and flowers
- Celebrate with feasting
- Honor peak solar power

**Lughnasadh (August 1)**
- Harvest first fruits
- Share food with community
- Compete in friendly contests
- Honor the grain god

**Mabon (Autumn Equinox)**
- Give thanks for harvest
- Balance work with rest
- Prepare for winter
- Reflect on the year's growth

## Closing Thoughts

Phil Carr-Gomm's work reminds us that Druidry is a living, breathing spiritual path that can enrich modern life. His emphasis on accessible practice, creative expression, and nature connection offers a way to engage with ancient wisdom without requiring adoption of rigid dogma or historical reenactment.

The practice is not about recreating the past, but about bringing the wisdom of the past into the present. When we connect with nature, express ourselves creatively, and engage with the stories that have shaped human consciousness, we discover that the spiritual path is not separate from daily life but woven through every moment of awareness and creation.

Carr-Gomm's legacy as Chosen Chief of OBOD has helped thousands find their way on the Druid path, showing that this ancient tradition has much to offer modern seekers looking for meaning, connection, and creative expression in their lives.
